About

I am a PhD Research Scholar in the Department of Aerospace Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras).

My research lies at the intersection of turbulence modelling, uncertainty quantification and computational fluid dynamics, with applications to aerospace flows.

I investigate epistemic uncertainty associated with turbulence closure models and develop methods to quantify and reduce uncertainty in Reynolds-averaged Navier–Stokes (RANS) predictions.
