About

I am a PhD Research Scholar in the Department of Aerospace Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras).

I am a SPARC-sponsored Researcher at the University of Melbourne, Victoria, Australia

My research lies at the intersection of turbulence modelling, uncertainty quantification and Machine Learning, with applications to aerospace flows.

I investigate the Epistemic uncertainty associated with turbulence closure models and develop methods to quantify and reduce uncertainty in Reynolds-averaged Navier–Stokes (RANS) predictions.
